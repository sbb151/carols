Jingle Bells

Jingle bells, jingle bells, Jingle all the way! Oh what fun it is to ride In a one-horse open sleigh, hey! Jingle bells, jingle bells, Jingle all the way! Oh what fun it is to ride In a one-horse open sleigh!

Dashing through the snow In a one-horse open sleigh, O'er the fields we go, Laughing all the way. Bells on bobtail ring, Making spirits bright, What fun it is to ride and sing A sleighing song tonight!
